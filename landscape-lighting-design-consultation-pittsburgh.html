Home/Project planning

Prepare for a landscape lighting design conversation.

You do not need a fixture list before speaking with Lumascapes. Start with the parts of your home you want to enjoy or see more clearly after dark. A useful design conversation connects those goals with the architecture, planting, access, and existing equipment at your Pittsburgh-area property.

Choose three evening priorities

Think about arrival, outdoor living, and views from indoors. For example, you may want a more welcoming front door, a readable route from the driveway, and a tree visible from the living room. Naming these outcomes helps separate the most useful work from additions that can wait.

Photograph the actual viewpoints

Take daylight photos from the street, the main walking route, and the seating area. Add nighttime photos showing existing lighting if you can do so safely. Include walls, steps, and mature planting that might affect installation. Photos are a starting point; access and equipment details may still require an on-site assessment.

Bring information about an existing system

Share accessible labels, approximate age, known faults, and any equipment documents. Mention planned landscape renovations, paving, or tree work. These changes can affect fixture positions and cable routes, so coordinating them early can prevent work that needs to be repeated.

Discuss scope and timing

Provide your location and preferred timing so availability can be confirmed. Explain your budget range if established and ask how a first phase would fit within it. Ask which parts of a proposal include installation, control setup, nighttime adjustments, and follow-up. No installation date or consultation price is promised by this guide.

Agree on the next decision

Before proceeding, ask what information is still needed, how the proposed design will be presented, and what the written estimate will cover. Keep optional additions separate so you can make a clear decision. Use the contact page to call or prepare a text message with your project details.

Common questions

Do I need to know which fixtures I want?

No. Describe the areas you want to improve and the nighttime effect you prefer. Fixture selection can follow the design discussion.

Can I request a conversation about existing lighting?

Yes. Describe whether you want diagnosis, a design refresh, or an expansion, and share any available equipment information.
