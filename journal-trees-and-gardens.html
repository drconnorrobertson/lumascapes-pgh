Journal / Trees and gardens

Trees and gardens ideas for after dark.

Plants are living architecture. A lighting plan should account for their shape today, how they grow, and the effect from the home as well as the yard.

Choose the question that fits your project.

These articles are planning resources for Pittsburgh-area homeowners. A property-specific design still begins with a conversation and a look at the setting.
