How to compare landscape lighting installation quotes.

The best way to compare landscape lighting installers is to ask each one to explain the same project. A front-entry installation and a whole-property design are different scopes even when both estimates say landscape lighting. Use this checklist before choosing a proposal for your Pittsburgh-area home.

Start with a shared design brief

Photograph the house from the street, the front walk, and the main backyard seating area. Mark what you want to see after dark and what currently bothers you. Give each installer the same priorities and identify any planned landscaping or hardscape work. Ask the proposal to distinguish essential work from optional additions.

Compare equipment details

Request fixture manufacturers or specifications, lamp or integrated-LED details, beam selection, and color temperature. Ask how the transformer and cable layout were selected. If one proposal substitutes equipment, ask what changes in appearance, serviceability, and warranty coverage. Product names alone do not explain whether the proposed design suits your home.

Clarify installation boundaries

Ask how existing walks, walls, planting beds, and decks affect access. Confirm who handles any required electrical work, permits, and restoration where applicable. The proposal should identify exclusions rather than leave you to discover them during installation. Do not assume a lighting estimate includes a new household electrical circuit or outlet.

Ask how the result will be adjusted

A fixture position selected in daylight may need refinement after dark. Ask whether nighttime aiming, control setup, and a walkthrough are included. Evaluate the view from inside the home as well as the curb. Clarify how to request an adjustment if planting growth or a seating change affects the scene later.

Keep the written record

Keep the final scope, approved changes, equipment list, payment schedule, and applicable warranty documents together. Ask who to contact for service and what information they will need. A clear record makes future maintenance and expansion easier, even if your original contact changes.

Common questions

What should I send when requesting a lighting estimate?

Send your location, photos, priorities, existing-system details, and preferred project timing. These help identify the scope before a property-specific estimate.

Should I choose the lowest landscape lighting quote?

First compare equivalent scopes. A lower total can reflect fewer illuminated areas, different equipment, omitted restoration, or fewer included adjustments.
