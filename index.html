Why the details matter

Lighting should feel as considered as the home itself.

One bright fixture can flatten a facade. A careful combination of soft light, direction, and shadow can reveal the texture of brick, the form of a tree, and the route to your door. The goal is a cohesive view, from the street and from the spaces you use every evening.

Light where it counts

Entrances, steps, paths, and gathering spaces deserve attention because they shape how the property is used.

Leave room for night

Subtle placement can build depth without turning the landscape into a floodlit scene.
