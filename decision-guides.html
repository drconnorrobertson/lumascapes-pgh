9 Checks for Pittsburgh Landscape Lighting Quotes

Landscape lighting quotes are difficult to compare when one lists fixtures and another describes a finished nighttime scene. Start with the routes and views you want to improve. Then ask each provider to explain how its proposal addresses those same priorities on your Pittsburgh-area property.

Solar vs Wired Low-Voltage Landscape Lighting

Solar fixtures and wired low-voltage systems suit different situations. Compare the job the light must perform, the conditions at its location and the maintenance you can support. A decorative accent in a sunny bed and a regularly used stepped route need different evaluation criteria.

Read the comparison and checklist

Repair vs Replace Existing Landscape Lighting

An older landscape lighting system does not need wholesale replacement simply because a few fixtures have stopped working. Start with a documented inspection and a nighttime review. The right scope could be a repair, a selective upgrade or a new plan, depending on what is failing and whether the current design still suits the property.
