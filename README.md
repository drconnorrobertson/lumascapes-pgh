# Lumascapes Pittsburgh website

Original static website for Lumascapes, a Pittsburgh-area landscape lighting brand.

## Pages

- `index.html` — home
- `services.html` — lighting services and FAQ
- `gallery.html` — visual inspiration gallery
- `about.html` — brand story
- `contact.html` — call, text, Instagram, and a text-preparation form

Open `index.html` locally or visit the [live GitHub Pages site](https://drconnorrobertson.github.io/lumascapes-pgh/). No build step or dependencies are required.

## Important content notes

- The six images in `assets/images` were generated for this website. They are **illustrative concepts**, not photographs of completed Lumascapes projects. The site labels them accordingly.
- The phone number `(412) 256-8351` appeared on a public Lumascapes Instagram promotional graphic and should be confirmed by the business before launch.
- The contact form prepares an SMS in the visitor's messaging app. The visitor must send it. No data is stored by this website.
- Confirm the business's exact service area, final offers, and any warranty or review claims before adding them.

## Publishing

GitHub Pages is enabled from the repository's `main` branch and `/ (root)` folder. Add a custom domain in Pages settings if one is available.

## Image prompts and provenance

Images were generated with the built-in image generation tool and optimized to JPEG for the website. All prompts requested premium, photorealistic architectural photography of Pittsburgh-style homes or outdoor spaces at dusk with warm low-voltage landscape lighting, no people, no text, no logos, and no watermark. Individual concepts:

- `hero.jpg`: stone and brick home on a wooded Pittsburgh hillside with layered facade, tree, step, and path lighting.
- `brick-home.jpg`: red brick home with mature oak trees, foundation planting, and path lighting.
- `walkway.jpg`: stone walkway on a gentle slope with tree and path lighting.
- `terrace.jpg`: intimate stone terrace with planting and step lights.
- `patio.jpg`: outdoor dining patio with garden accents and blue-hour skyline.
- `stone-home.jpg`: historic-style stone home with architectural lighting and entry steps.
