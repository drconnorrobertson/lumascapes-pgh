Privacy

This website does not store information you type into the contact form. Submitting the form prepares a text message in your device's messaging app; you review and send the message yourself. Standard messaging and carrier practices may apply once you send it.

Calling, texting, or messaging Lumascapes through Instagram may share the information you choose to provide with those services and with Lumascapes. Do not send sensitive personal information through the website.

This site uses basic browser code and publicly hosted files. It does not currently use an advertising pixel or analytics tracker. External sites linked from this website have their own privacy practices.

Website terms

Content on this website is for general information and inspiration. Design ideas, availability, and project details depend on a conversation about your property. Nothing on this site is a quote, warranty, or promise of a specific result.

Images and visual concepts

Some images on this website are AI-generated visual concepts created to illustrate possible landscape lighting moods and settings. They are not photographs of completed Lumascapes installations. Lumascapes plans to add real project photography as it becomes available. Captions in the gallery identify these images as illustrative concepts.

Contact

For questions about this website or a lighting project, contact Lumascapes or call (412) 256-8351.
