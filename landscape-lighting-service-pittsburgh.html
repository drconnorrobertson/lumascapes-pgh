Landscape Lighting Service for Pittsburgh-area homes

Even a strong lighting design can need attention over time. Plantings grow, fixtures move, connections age, and the way you use an outdoor space can change. Lumascapes offers landscape lighting service for Pittsburgh-area homeowners who want to revisit an existing system.

Every property and project is different. Our conversation begins with what you want to see, how you use the space, and which parts of the home or landscape deserve attention after dark.

Start with what is happening now

Are some fixtures dark? Has the balance changed as trees and shrubs grew? Does one area feel too bright while another feels forgotten? Describe the system and what you notice, and we can discuss the appropriate next step.

Adjust the design as the landscape changes

Outdoor lighting is part of a living setting. A fixture that once highlighted a young tree may need to be repositioned later. A patio addition or a new path may call for the lighting plan to expand or shift.

Preserve the whole composition

Service is about more than a single fixture. It is also an opportunity to check whether the overall effect still feels coherent from the spaces you use most: the street, the walkway, and the view from inside.

The best lighting gives every evening a more considered point of view.

What a first conversation can cover

  • The spaces you want to use, highlight, or navigate after sunset
  • How the lighting should look from the street and from inside the home
  • Whether an existing system is present and what you would like to change
  • Your location in the Pittsburgh area and the next practical step

Common questions

Can Lumascapes look at a system someone else installed?

Tell us about the existing system when you reach out. We can discuss its condition and whether it is a fit for service.

What information should I send?

Your town, the age of the system if known, a short description of the issue, and photos if available are helpful starting points.

Can my current lighting design be refreshed?

Potentially. An update may involve adjustments, replacement of individual elements, or a broader redesign depending on the condition and goals.
